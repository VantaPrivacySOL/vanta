# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want VANTA to be a project anyone can take part in without harassment, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## Our standards

What helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experience.
- Giving and taking constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use and build on the project.

What is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Promoting tokens in issues and pull requests, or labelling other people's projects as scams without evidence.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through the repository's private vulnerability reporting form on the Security tab, marked as a conduct report. Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and may remove comments, close threads or block accounts that break these rules.
